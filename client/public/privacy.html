Remindly

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

Remindly is a personal-project voice-reminder calendar app. This page explains what data it collects, why, and how it's used — plainly, without legal boilerplate padding it out.

Signing in

Remindly uses Google Sign-In (via Firebase Authentication) as its only way to create an account. When you sign in, Google shares your name, email address, and profile photo with Firebase Authentication so your session can be identified — but Remindly's own database never stores your name or email. Internally, the app only keeps your account's unique ID, which is what every event and assistant you create is tied to.

Google Calendar (optional)

On first login, Remindly asks whether you'd like to import your existing Google Calendar events. If you say yes, it requests read-only access to your calendar events (the calendar.events.readonly scope) — just enough to fetch your events once and copy them in. Remindly never creates, edits, or deletes anything on your actual Google Calendar, and never requests broader access than this. You can decline this step entirely and use the app without it.

What Remindly stores

Everything you create in the app — events (title, time, location, description, color, reminder timing), and "assistants" (a name, a role/emotion persona, a chosen voice, and an optional uploaded photo) — is stored in Firebase (Google Cloud infrastructure) under your account's unique ID, and is only ever readable by your own account.

AI-generated reminders

When a reminder is due, Remindly sends the event's details and the assistant's persona to Google's Gemini API (server-side, never directly from your browser) to generate a short spoken script and synthesize it into audio. That audio is stored alongside your event and deleted automatically if you delete the event.

What Remindly does not do

  • Does not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing.
  • Does not show ads or use analytics/tracking scripts.
  • Does not write to, modify, or delete anything in your actual Google Calendar.

Deleting your data

Deleting an event or an assistant inside the app permanently removes it, including any generated audio or uploaded photo tied to it. To delete your entire account and all associated data, email patjandra@berkeley.edu and it will be removed.

Children

Remindly is not directed at, and is not knowingly used by, children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change with it.

Contact
